# Release scope and attribution

This is a public-source candidate for a 108-row experimental catalog, not a blanket rights clearance for music or model training data.

FMA metadata is attributed to Michaël Defferrard, Kirell Benzi, Pierre Vandergheynst and Xavier Bresson, *FMA: A Dataset For Music Analysis*, ISMIR 2017. The upstream [FMA repository](https://github.com/mdeff/fma) identifies metadata as [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Metadata was selected and reformatted for this interface.

The selected source recordings are recorded as 31 CC BY 4.0, 62 CC BY 3.0 Unported and 15 CC0 tracks. Each catalog row retains the creator, title, source, license URL, supplied notices and modification account. [The credits page](notices/track-attribution.html) displays all 108 rows. The applicable terms permit public sharing, subject to their conditions; private-only language in the earlier experiment was its publication scope, not a restriction in these licenses. See [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/legalcode.en), and [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en).

No audio is distributed here. Catalog audio paths, lengths and digests are retained as provenance and do not identify downloadable files in this repository. Do not enable playback links until the separately hosted audio and audience have been checked. Original FMA excerpts were approximately 30 seconds; their offsets within the source recordings are unknown. The vectors are new CLAP computations using fixed 0/10/20-second windows, per-window normalization, mean pooling and final normalization. Per-track attribution is retained conservatively for these derivatives. This does not assert that all embeddings are copyright-free or relicense the original recordings.

The Q8 text model is sourced, unchanged, from `Xenova/clap-htsat-unfused` at revision `c28f2883575e590e04d3146ff0713c2448d691ba`. It is absent from Git and fetched only by the explicit build step. The recorded upstream grant is the LAION model card's Apache-2.0 declaration. Full terms, both model cards, fairseq/RoBERTa MIT attribution and relevant runtime/ancestry notices are retained under `notices/`. The model's exact conversion build and full training-corpus rights have not been independently audited. Converter software licenses do not relicense musical works.

The recovered dependency notices cover the exact previously installed versions; a fresh image must still inventory its actual wheels and system components. The ONNX Runtime third-party notice is an upstream source superset, not a claim that every listed component ships. No artist, model author or dataset contributor endorsement is implied.

The source assessment found no additional public-release permission requirement in these recorded CC BY/CC0 grants for the narrowly exported catalog/vectors. This conclusion relies on the existing per-track source assertions and preserves their qualifications; it is not a guarantee of all underlying rights. A new corpus, model, license class or public audio distribution needs its own review and attribution. Project-authored code has no newly chosen general reuse license in this export.
