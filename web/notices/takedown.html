Rights and removal requests

Music Discovery Atlas is a non-commercial research demonstration. It plays 30-second excerpts of recordings that their creators published under Creative Commons licences on the Free Music Archive, with a credit for each on the credits page. It has no advertising, sponsors, payments, accounts or user uploads.

If something here is yours, or something is wrong, write to [RIGHTS CONTACT: set MUSIC_RIGHTS_CONTACT before this page is published]. The inbox is read every business day. Please include the recording's credit link, which ends in an identifier such as fma-141074, or its title and artist.

What you can ask us to do

Take a recording down. If you made or own a recording here and want it removed, tell us and we will remove it. You don't need to give a reason.

Change or remove your credit. You can ask us to remove your name, copyright notice or source link from a credit, or to credit you differently: another name, a pseudonym, a link, or a role such as "producer". Creative Commons licences give you this right (CC BY 4.0, section 3(a)(3)), and we honour it for every licence version. We keep the licence name and link, because the licence requires them.

Fix a mistake. A wrong title, name or licence, or a recording that isn't what its credit says.

Tell us about rights that weren't licensed. If a recording here contains your work (a song you wrote, a sample of your recording, a performance, or an upload by someone who had no right to license it), tell us what the work is and how it is yours.

Send a formal copyright notice. We accept notices under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3)). A notice should include:

  1. your physical or electronic signature;
  2. the copyrighted work you say is infringed;
  3. the material on this site you say infringes it, with its credit link;
  4. your address, telephone number and email address;
  5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law;
  6. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

Knowingly and materially misrepresenting that material is infringing can make you liable for damages (17 U.S.C. §512(f)). You don't need a formal notice for any of the other requests: an ordinary email is enough.

What happens next

  • We reply within 2 business days.
  • Unless you have only asked for a credit change, we switch the recording off (search, map, player and its audio address) within 2 business days, usually the same day, while we look into it. We don't wait to decide who is right first.
  • Credit changes reach every place this site shows the credit: the credits page and the credit links beside each result and in the player.
  • We tell you what we did.
  • We put a recording back only when the question is settled in writing, for example when the rights holder confirms the licence.
  • If you are the credited artist and your recording was taken down because of someone else's claim, write to us and we will tell you what was claimed and how it can be settled.
  • If a source turns out to have mislabelled rights, we stop adding recordings from it and recheck the ones we have.

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding US federal holidays, US Eastern time.

Copies elsewhere

Removing a recording here doesn't remove copies on other sites. The Free Music Archive's copyright contact is complaints@freemusicarchive.org; the Internet Archive's is info@archive.org. This site is hosted by Render, which handles notices about content on its servers under its own DMCA policy.

Your information

We use what you send only to handle your request, and keep a private record of each request and what we did. We may tell the credited artist or the source platform what was claimed so the question can be settled; we won't publish your contact details.

Recordings withdrawn after a request

None so far. Recordings that our own rights review holds back are not on this list; they are simply not served.
